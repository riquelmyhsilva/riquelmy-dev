## Overview & Problem

Sentiment classification on full-length movie reviews requires capturing semantic nuance, negation, and long-range context — areas where traditional *bag-of-words* models often fall short.

This project implements an end-to-end **Natural Language Processing (NLP)** pipeline that fine-tunes a **Transformer (BERT)** model on the **IMDB** dataset, covering data preparation, tokenization, supervised fine-tuning, metric evaluation, and inference.

## Architecture & Solution

The pipeline is organized into four main stages:

- **Data Preparation & Tokenization**: loads the IMDB dataset, cleans raw review text, and applies BERT-compatible tokenization (including padding, truncation, and attention masks).
- **Supervised Fine-Tuning**: adapts pre-trained BERT weights for binary sentiment classification using **PyTorch** and the **Hugging Face** ecosystem.
- **Metric Evaluation**: tracks training/validation loss and classification metrics on held-out test splits to assess generalization.
- **Inference Pipeline**: exposes a clean workflow to run sentiment predictions on custom, unseen review texts.

## Tech Stack & Technical Decisions

- **Python & PyTorch**: granular control over the training loop, tensor operations, and hardware acceleration (GPU).
- **BERT & Hugging Face Transformers**: leverages bidirectional pre-trained representations (*transfer learning*) to achieve strong semantic understanding with practical training budgets.
- **Modular Experiment Structure**: structured to make hyperparameter tuning (learning rate, batch size, epochs, and maximum sequence length) straightforward and reproducible.

## Results & Key Takeaways

- Demonstrates the complete *transfer learning* lifecycle for modern NLP using *Self-Attention* architectures.
- Serves as a reusable blueprint for text classification, automated feedback triage, and opinion mining tasks.
