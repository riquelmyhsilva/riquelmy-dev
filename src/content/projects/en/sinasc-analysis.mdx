## Overview & Problem

Public healthcare datasets such as Brazil's **SINASC (Live Birth Information System)** hold valuable insights into demographic and maternal-infant health indicators, but raw records often involve inconsistent data types, missing values, and complex categorical encodings.

This project builds a data cleaning and **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** pipeline over SINASC records, turning raw public datasets into structured indicators and clear visual analyses.

## Architecture & Solution

The analytical workflow is structured into reproducible notebook stages:

- **Ingestion & Data Cleaning**: loads raw records, handles missing values, standardizes categorical codes, and enforces consistent data types.
- **Feature Engineering & Aggregation**: derives analytical variables and groups records across temporal and regional dimensions to compute maternal and neonatal indicators.
- **Exploratory Analysis & Visualization**: produces summary tables and charts to inspect distributions, trends, and relationships across demographic and clinical variables.

## Tech Stack & Technical Decisions

- **Python & Pandas**: vectorized tabular data manipulation, grouping/pivoting (`groupby`, `pivot_table`), and clean handling of categorical and time-based attributes.
- **Jupyter Notebooks**: combines code, cleaning assumptions, and visual interpretations in a transparent, step-by-step analytical narrative.

## Results & Key Takeaways

- Provides a clean, repeatable workflow for preparing Brazilian public health microdata for statistical analysis.
- Highlights practical data wrangling and visual storytelling techniques for real-world tabular datasets.
